The mission

12 Jyotirlinga Solo Road Trip

This route is designed as a long-form India road trip starting from the NCR/Ghaziabad side, moving through the Himalayas, the Ganga belt, eastern India, south India, Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, and Gujarat.

Destination map

Shrines Across India

Use the map as the visual anchor, then follow the planned order below for a road-friendly solo loop.

  1. KedarnathUttarakhand - mountain drive plus trek buffer
  2. Kashi VishwanathVaranasi, Uttar Pradesh - ghats, darshan, local lanes
  3. BaidyanathDeoghar, Jharkhand - eastern India temple halt
  4. MallikarjunaSrisailam, Andhra Pradesh - forest road and dam belt
  5. RameshwaramTamil Nadu - sea bridge, Dhanushkodi, Ramanathaswamy Temple
  6. BhimashankarMaharashtra - Western Ghats road and trek-like terrain
  7. TrimbakeshwarNashik, Maharashtra - Godavari origin region
  8. GrishneshwarKhuldabad, Maharashtra - near Ellora caves
  9. OmkareshwarMadhya Pradesh - Narmada island temple town
  10. MahakaleshwarUjjain, Madhya Pradesh - plan early for Bhasma Aarti
  11. SomnathVeraval, Gujarat - coast, sunset, temple light show
  12. NageshwarDwarka, Gujarat - combine with Dwarkadhish and Bet Dwarka

On-road planning

Make The Journey Filmable And Practical

Route rhythm

Keep long highway days between 450 and 650 km, then slow down near temples, ghats, mountains, and coastal stretches.

Budget notes

Track fuel, tolls, parking, darshan booking, food, dormitories, hotels, bike service, and emergency cash separately.

Vlog moments

Capture start-of-day plans, road conditions, stay reviews, temple approach, local food, and honest solo-travel lessons.

Safety buffer

Keep rest days after mountain and night-drive stretches, share live location, and avoid pushing through fatigue.
